## Objective
Resolve each review finding on its merits. Act on a finding that is right, and answer one that is not.

## Finding Rules
- **Fix** the cause of a finding, not its symptom.
- **Answer** a finding that is wrong, or whose case cannot occur, with the reason. Do not change the work for it.
- **Leave** a finding that is a nitpick, asks for work outside this artifact, or belongs in a follow-up. Say which it is and why.
- **Check** whether the model is wrong before you add a guard, a flag, a state field, or a config key. If it is, change the model.

## Out of Scope
- **Do not grow** the scope of the artifact to satisfy a finding.
